# User guide

The Command Center ranks issues by risk, business impact, evidence confidence, and urgency. Open a finding to inspect affected resources and evidence before accepting, assigning, or drafting remediation. A recommendation is not proof that an automated action is safe for your organization; policy and approval remain authoritative.

Security posture is a local composite measure and should be interpreted with its component controls and trend. Collection freshness is shown alongside results; stale sources reduce confidence. License savings are estimates until contract prices, user exceptions, leave status, and service accounts are validated.

AI Analyst answers only from data your role may access and should cite local evidence. Treat generated summaries and workflows as drafts. Report suspected prompt injection, unexpected disclosure, or an unsupported claim to a Platform Administrator and preserve the interaction identifier.
