# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different viewpoints.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus discussion on evidence, the product, and the community.
- Respect privacy: never publish customer data, credentials, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private information, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the mechanism in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), clearly stating that the report concerns conduct. Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or otherwise act on contributions and comments that violate this Code. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
